Under the Basket

Timed drills for all five positions. Pick a programme, work through the levels, tap Hit or Miss on every rep and save the score. Tap Show me on a drill to see it on a court with what to watch for. Build your own from the drill pool when none of the programmes fit.

    New programme

    Build the programme from drills in the library, from drills you write yourself, or both. Each score counts hits out of the reps you set, and in a session you tap Hit or Miss for every rep. A programme can have up to 12 drills, each with up to 3 scores of up to 20 reps.

    Drill library

    Add drills to your programme. Each one is copied, so you can change it without touching the original. Show me plays the drill on a court.
